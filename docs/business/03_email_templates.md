## ✅ Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Guidelines
- Keep under 50 characters
- Ask a question or create curiosity
- Avoid spam triggers (FREE, !!!, ALL CAPS)
- A/B test with 2-3 variations

### Body Guidelines
- Personalize with merge tags
- Keep paragraphs to 1-3 lines
- Include clear CTA (one primary)
- Mobile-friendly formatting
- Personal sign-off (not "The Team")

### Sending Best Practices
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- Best times: 9-11am, 2-4pm local time
- Avoid Mondays (inbox overload) and Fridays (checked out)
- Space follow-ups 3-7 days apart
