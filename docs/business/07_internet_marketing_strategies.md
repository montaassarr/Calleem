## 📧 4. Email Marketing

### Email List Building

#### Lead Magnet Strategy
| Magnet | Target Audience | Conversion Rate |
|--------|-----------------|-----------------|
| ROI Calculator | Cost-conscious buyers | 8-12% |
| Industry Guide PDF | Researching solutions | 5-8% |
| Comparison Checklist | Late-stage prospects | 10-15% |
| Free Consultation | High-intent leads | 15-20% |

#### Opt-In Placements
1. **Exit Intent Popup**: Offer lead magnet when leaving
2. **Blog Sidebar**: Newsletter signup
3. **Content Upgrades**: Downloadable versions of blog posts
4. **Pricing Page**: "Get Quote" form
5. **Chat Widget**: Email capture before demo

### Email Sequences

#### 1. Welcome Sequence (New Subscriber)
- Day 0: Welcome + what to expect
- Day 2: Best content piece
- Day 5: Problem awareness
- Day 7: Solution introduction
- Day 10: Demo CTA

#### 2. Demo Nurture Sequence (Requested Demo)
- Immediately: Confirmation + what to expect
- Day 1: (If no-show) Reschedule offer
- Day 2: Value content
- Day 5: Social proof + urgency
- Day 10: Final offer

#### 3. Trial/Onboarding Sequence
- Day 1: Welcome + setup guide
- Day 3: First call celebration
- Day 7: Tips for success
- Day 10: Feature highlight
- Day 13: Upgrade CTA

#### Newsletter Strategy (Weekly)
- **Monday send**: Highest open rates for B2B
- **Content**: Tips + customer story + product update + industry news
- **Goal**: Stay top of mind, drive demo bookings

### Online Communities to Engage

| Platform | Communities | Strategy |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Reddit | r/smallbusiness, r/startups, r/entrepreneur | Value-first answers |
| Facebook | Dental practice owners groups, SMB groups | Helpful posts |

### Rules for Community Engagement
1. **Never spam or self-promote** (get banned immediately)
2. **Add genuine value first** (answer questions helpfully)
3. **Build relationships** (people buy from people they trust)
4. **Mention Calleem only when directly relevant** (and asked)
5. **Share wins, not sales pitches**

## 📈 9. Analytics & Optimization

### Tracking Stack
| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Google Analytics 4 | Website behavior |
| Google Tag Manager | Event tracking |
| Hotjar/FullStory | Heatmaps, session recordings |
| HubSpot/Salesforce | CRM pipeline |
| Facebook Pixel | Conversion tracking |
| LinkedIn Insight Tag | Ad tracking |
| UTM Parameters | Channel attribution |

### Key Metrics to Track

#### Website Metrics
| Metric | Target | Action if Off-Target |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| Bounce rate | < 50% | Improve content relevance |
| Time on site | > 2 min | Add engaging content |
| Pages per session | > 2 | Improve internal linking |
| Demo conversion | > 5% | Optimize CTA placement |

#### Ad Metrics
| Metric | Target | Action if Off-Target |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| CTR (Search) | > 3% | Improve ad copy |
| CTR (Social) | > 1% | Improve creative |
| CPC | < $5 | Refine targeting |
| Cost per demo | < $100 | Optimize funnel |
| ROAS | > 5:1 | Cut underperformers |

### A/B Testing Priority
1. Landing page headlines
2. CTA button text and color
3. Demo booking form length
4. Email subject lines
5. Ad creative variations
