# 📘 CallFlow AI - User Guide

Welcome to **CallFlow AI**! This guide will help you set up your AI receptionist and start automating your business in minutes.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up & Onboarding
1. Create an account at [app.callflowai.com](https://app.callflowai.com).
2. Complete the **Onboarding Wizard**:
   - Enter your Business Name.
   - Select your Industry (e.g., Barbershop, Dental, Legal).
   - Choose your AI's Voice & Personality.

### 2. Get Your Phone Number
1. Go to **Settings > Integrations**.
2. Connect your **Twilio Account** (or buy a number directly through us).
3. Once connected, your AI is live! Call your new number to test it.

### 3. Add Your Services
1. Go to **Dashboard > Services**.
2. Add your service menu (e.g., "Men's Haircut - 30 mins - $40").
3. The AI will strictly follow this menu when booking appointments.

---

## ⚡ Smart Automations (Pro Plan)

Unlock the full power of automation with the **Pro Plan ($499/mo)**.

### How to Enable:
1. Go to **Dashboard > Smart Automations**.
2. Toggle **ON** the integrations you need:
   - **📅 Google Calendar**: Instantly sync bookings to your calendar.
   - **📊 Airtable**: Log every call transcript to a spreadsheet.
   - **💬 WhatsApp/SMS**: Send automatic booking confirmations.
   - **👥 HubSpot**: Create contacts from new callers.
   - **📢 Slack**: Notify your team channel when a booking is made.
3. Click **Save Automations**.

**That's it!** Our system automatically builds and deploys a custom workflow for your business.

---

## 📞 Managing Calls

- **Live Dashboard**: See calls coming in real-time on the **Control Room** page.
- **Call Logs**: Review transcripts and audio recordings in **Call Logs**.
- **Appointments**: View and manage all AI-booked slots in the **Appointments** calendar.
